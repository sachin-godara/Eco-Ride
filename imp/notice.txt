Important Notice – NSP Minor Project

Dear Students,

Please note that your NSP Minor Project should be developed with proper understanding of the project, code, and technologies used.

Simply copy-pasting code generated by AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI tools and submitting the project will not be sufficient for evaluation. You are expected to understand the working, logic, functionality, and implementation of your project.

Also, some students are developing/running their programs only on online AI platforms such as Claude. Such projects will not be considered for evaluation. Your complete project must be properly configured and runnable on your own local system.

This is important because Viva will be conducted during Mid Term-I, and you may be asked questions related to:

* Your project functionality and workflow
* Code and logic implemented
* Java Swing GUI
* MySQL database and connectivity
* Project design and flowchart
* Technologies and concepts used in your project

You must be able to explain and demonstrate your project yourself. If you are unable to run the project locally or explain its implementation during the viva, it will directly affect your evaluation.

Note: AI tools may be used as learning/support tools, but the final project must be understood, implemented, tested, and demonstrated by you.

Please take this seriously and prepare your project accordingly.
